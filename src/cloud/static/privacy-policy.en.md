# Agent Limbo Privacy Policy (English)

Last Updated: 2026-07-22
Effective Date: 2026-07-22

Welcome to Agent Limbo (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how YUCHUAN GAN ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to information processing related to your use of Agent Limbo features on iOS and watchOS.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process the following information:

1. Account Information (provided with your authorization)
- Sign-in method (for example, Sign in with Apple)
- Display name
- Email address (depending on your Apple authorization settings)

2. Device and App Information
- Device-related information (for example, device model and OS version)
- App version and build number
- Push notification device token (APNs device token)

3. Pairing and Connection Configuration
- Pairing code and pairing status
- Service host address
- Authentication token (auth token)

4. Operational and Usage Information
- Approval actions (such as approve, deny, retry, skip)
- Recent message summaries and status display information

Note: Some of the above information may be stored locally on your device only, for feature enablement and user experience.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use your information only as necessary to provide the App's functionality, including:

1. Account identification and cross-device sync
2. Pairing between watch, phone, and/or backend services
3. Push notifications and status alerts
4. Approval workflow interactions and history display
5. Troubleshooting and service security

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when:

1. You provide explicit authorization or consent
2. Sharing is necessary with service providers to deliver core features (for example, Apple Push Notification service)
3. Required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request
4. Needed to protect legal rights, safety, and security

## 5. Data Storage and Security

1. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data.
2. Some configuration data (such as host, token, and pairing status) may be stored locally on your device to maintain connection and usability.
3. No method of transmission or storage is absolutely secure.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You may request deletion or discontinuation by uninstalling the App, resetting configuration, or contacting support.

## 7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to:

1. Access and correct your personal information
2. Request deletion of your personal information
3. Withdraw consent (without affecting prior lawful processing)
4. Disable notification permissions or stop account sync

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the age required by your jurisdiction). If we learn we collected children's data unintentionally, we will delete it promptly.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The App may integrate with third-party services (for example, Apple account services and Apple Push services). Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 10. Policy Updates

We may update this Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted in-app or on our website. For material changes, we will provide reasonable notice.
